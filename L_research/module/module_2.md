## Lesson 1.1: "Mama" Recognition and Reinforcement
**Word Target: "Mama"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Connect "mama" sound to primary caregiver**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Emotional Connection (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "Who loves you? Mama!" with warm tone
- Visual Element: Heart grows larger when "mama" is said
- Baby's Job: Listen and observe emotional connection

- **Phase 2**: Recognition Practice (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: Any attempt at "mama", "ma", "mum"
- Visual Response: Multiple hearts float with gentle chime
- Special Feature: Extra celebration for clear "mama"
- Interactive Element: "Where is mama?" prompts

- **Phase 3**: Meaning Reinforcement (60 seconds)
- Achievement Response: Mama celebration animation
- Buddy's Role: "You called mama! She's so happy!"
- Real-world Connection: Encourage mama to respond immediately

## Lesson 1.2: "Dada" Sound-Person Association
**Word Target: "Dada"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Associate "dada" with father figure**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Person Connection (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "Let's call dada!" with exciting tone
- Visual Element: Strong, stable shapes (squares/triangles)
- Context: Show dada's activities or characteristics

- **Phase 2**: Call and Response (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "Dada", "da", "daddy" attempts
- Visual Response: Shapes build into strong structures
- Special Response: Building blocks stack for "dada"
- Interactive Game: "Call dada!" followed by waiting

- **Phase 3**: Connection Celebration (60 seconds)
- Achievement: Blocks create a house or tower
- Message: "Dada heard you! He's coming!"
- Real-world Cue: Signal for dada to appear/respond

## Lesson 1.3: "Bye-Bye" Gesture-Sound Connection
**Word Target: "Bye-Bye"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Combine gesture and sound for social interaction**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Gesture Introduction (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: Waves while saying "bye-bye"
- Visual Element: Hand animation waves in sync with sound

- **Phase 2**: Combined Practice (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "Bye", waving gesture, or both
- Visual Response: Multiple hands wave across screen
- Sound Recognition: Extra celebration for "bye" sounds
- Success Marker: Any combination of wave + sound attempt

- **Phase 3**: Social Connection (60 seconds)
- Achievement: Hands wave in patterns with musical goodbye
- Buddy's Role: "See you soon! Bye-bye!"


## Lesson 2.1: "Up" Request Recognition
**Word Target: "Up"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Express desire to be lifted or reach higher**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Action Demonstration (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: Shows upward movement with "up!"
- Visual Element: Arrow rises while "up" is spoken
- Physical Context: Associate with being lifted

- **Phase 2**: Request Practice (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "Up", "uh", reaching gestures
- Visual Response: Arrow lifts objects on screen
- Gesture Recognition: Reaching arms upward


## Lesson 2.2: "More" Want Expression
**Word Target: "More"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Request continuation of enjoyable activities**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Desired Communication (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "Want more?" with anticipation
- Visual Element: Plus sign appears with "more"
- Context: More food, more play, more fun
- Teaching: You can ask for what you want

- **Phase 2**: Request Building (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "More", "mor", "muh", clapping
- Visual Response: Plus signs multiply on screen
- Gesture Recognition: Finger tapping or clapping
- Combination Rewards: Sound + gesture = bigger celebrationnnnnn

- **Phase 3**: Satisfaction Response (60 seconds)
- Achievement: Plus signs create abundance visual
- Buddy's Role: "More coming! Good asking!"
- Application: Perfect for meals and play time

## Lesson 2.3: "Go" Movement Connection
**Word Target: "Go"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Initiate or request movement and action**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Movement Initiation (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "Ready, set, GO!" with energy
- Visual Element: Race car starts moving on "go"
- Energy Building: Anticipation then action
- Movement Association: Go means start moving

- **Phase 2**: Action Triggers (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "Go", "guh", forward movement gestures
- Visual Response: Vehicles race across screen
- Gesture Recognition: Pointing forward, crawling motions
- Energy Matching: Faster sounds = faster movement
- Success Marker: Baby initiates movement or sound

- **Phase 3**: Movement Celebration (60 seconds)
- Achievement: Multiple vehicles create action scene
- Buddy's Role: "Go, go, go! You're moving!"
- Physical Encouragement: Perfect for crawling/walking practice

## Lesson 3.1: "Ball" Object Identification
**Word Target: "Ball"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Connect word to specific object**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Object Introduction (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "This is a ball!" while showing ball
- Visual Element: Ball bounces on screen
- Physical Connection: Use real ball if available
- Shape Recognition: Round objects are balls

- **Phase 2**: Identification Practice (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "Ball", "bah", "ba"
- Visual Response: Balls of different colors bounce
- Object Recognition: Point to ball, touch ball
- Word Association: Ball sound = round bouncy thing

## Lesson 3.2: "Book" Activity Association
**Word Target: "Book"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Associate word with reading activity**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Object and Activity (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "Let's read a book!" while showing book
- Visual Element: Book opens to reveal colorful pages
- Activity Association: Books are for looking and learning
- Quiet Time Connection: Books for calm moments

- **Phase 2**: Recognition and Interest (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "Book", "buh", "boo"
- Visual Response: Books open revealing pictures
- Physical Recognition: Looking at books, turning pages
- Story Connection: Books contain interesting things
- Success Marker: Baby shows interest in books when word is said

## Lesson 3.3: "Water" Need Communication
**Word Target: "Water"**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Express basic need for hydration**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Need Recognition (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "Do you want water?" with cup gesture
- Visual Element: Water flows gently on screen
- Basic Need: Water for when thirsty
- Health Connection: Water helps us feel good

- **Phase 2**: Request Practice (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: "Water", "wah", "wa", reaching for cup
- Visual Response: Water creates refreshing animations
- Gesture Recognition: Reaching toward water or cup
- Need Communication: Asking for what body needs
- Success Marker: Clear request for water

## Lesson 3.4: Personal Name Recognition
**Word Target: Baby's Own Name**
**Duration: 4-5 minutes**
**Learning Goal: Recognize and respond to personal name**

### Lesson Structure:
- **Phase 1**: Identity Introduction (60 seconds)
- Buddy's Role: "[Baby's name]! That's YOU!"
- Visual Element: Special star appears with baby's name
- Self Recognition: This sound means YOU
- Identity Building: You are special and important

- **Phase 2**: Name Response (180 seconds)
- Sound Trigger: Baby's name spoken clearly
- Visual Response: Personal star grows bright and special
- Recognition Signs: Looking up, smiling, turning toward voice
- Identity Connection: Your name = your attention
- Success Marker: Baby responds when name is called
