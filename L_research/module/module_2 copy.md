## Interactive Activities

### Activity 1: Alpha Block Sorting
**Materials**: Printed alpha blocks, word cards
**Process**:
1. Call out words (bat, map, shut, nap, gap)
2. Students hold up the alpha block that starts that word
3. Celebrate correct answers
4. Repeat with different words

### Activity 2: Word Building Race
**Materials**: Alpha blocks, word list cards
**Process**:
1. Show a word card (e.g., "bat")
2. Students race to arrange blocks to spell the word
3. First one correct wins a point
4. Rotate through different words

### Activity 3: Sound Story Creation
**Materials**: Alpha blocks and objects that match sounds
**Process**:
1. Select 2-3 sounds (e.g., ba, ma, na)
2. Create simple story: "The **bat** flew to the **mat** to see the **nap**"
3. Use actions and voice changes
4. Have students repeat the story

### Activity 4: Spelling with "Shh"
**Materials**: Alpha blocks, worksheets
**Process**:
1. Say a "shh" word aloud (e.g., "shop")
2. Students spell it using alpha blocks
3. Write it on the worksheet
4. Draw a picture of the word
5. Read it back aloud

### Activity 5: Sound Matching Game
**Materials**: Picture cards, alpha blocks
**Process**:
1. Display picture card (e.g., a bat, a map, a ship)
2. Students match the starting sound with correct alpha block
3. Say the word aloud together
4. Progress to more difficult words

## 📝 Weekly Lesson Structure

### Day 1: Sound Introduction
- Introduce one new sound (ba, na, tha, ga, or ma)
- Isolated sound practice (10 minutes)
- Display alpha block prominently
- Read 3-4 words starting with that sound

### Day 2: Sound Recognition
- Review sound from Day 1
- Point and listen activity
- Rhyming words practice
- Introduction to "shh" (once other sounds are established)

### Day 3: Word Building
- Blend 3-5 CVC words with the sound
- Write words on board while blending
- Students trace and write words
- Picture-word matching

### Day 4: Practice and Reinforcement
- All activities from Days 1-3
- Spelling exercises
- Reading simple sentences with the sound
- Special "shh" practice (if focusing on that week)

### Day 5: Review and Assessment
- Review all sounds learned so far
- Spelling quiz (3-5 words per sound)
- Sound Hunt in picture books
- Celebration of progress
