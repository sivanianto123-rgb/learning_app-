## Baby Learning App Syllabus (0-12 Months)
## Level 1: Sound Recognition & Response (0-3 Months)
### Week 1-2: Voice & Sound Recognition
## Learning Objective: Baby recognizes and responds to sounds

- App plays gentle voice recordings saying baby's name
- Soft lullabies and musical sounds
- Simple sound patterns (tick-tock, whoosh, ring-ring)
- Baby responds by looking at screen or making sounds

### Week 3-4: Visual-Audio Connection
## Learning Objective: Connect sounds with visual elements

- Colorful shapes appear with corresponding sounds
- Musical notes with gentle melodies
- Simple animations with sound effects
- Baby tracks moving objects with eyes

### Week 5-6: Cooing Interaction
## Learning Objective: Encourage baby's first vocalizations

- App responds to baby's sounds with visual rewards
- Gentle "coo-coo" sounds with animated characters
- Long vowel sounds: "Ahhh," "Ohhh," "Eeee"
- Baby attempts to imitate sounds

### Week 7-8: Turn-Taking Games
## Learning Objective: Establish sound-response patterns

- App makes sound, waits, then responds to baby's sound
- "Peek-a-boo" games with sound and visual surprise
- Simple call-and-response activities
- Baby learns to wait and respond

### Week 9-10: Rhythm & Pitch Recognition
## Learning Objective: Distinguish different sound qualities

- High and low pitch sounds with different colored visuals
- Rhythmic patterns with bouncing animations
- Musical scales with ascending/descending visuals
- Baby shows preference for certain sounds

### Week 11-12: Vowel Sound Practice
## Learning Objective: Clear vowel sound recognition

- Individual vowel sounds with matching mouth animations
- A-E-I-O-U sequence with colorful letters
- Vowel sounds paired with simple object images
- Baby attempts vowel-like sounds

## Level 2: Sound Production & Babbling (3-6 Months)
### Week 13-14: First Consonant Sounds
## Learning Objective: Produce basic consonant sounds

- "Ma-ma-ma" with animated mouth movements
- "Ba-ba-ba" with bouncing ball visuals
- "Da-da-da" with dancing characters
Baby attempts consonant sounds

### Week 15-16: Consonant-Vowel Combinations
## Learning Objective: Combine consonants with vowels

- "Mama" with mother character animation
- "Baba" with bottle or ball objects
- "Dada" with father character animation
Baby produces CV combinations

### Week 17-18: Extended Consonant Practice
## Learning Objective: Expand consonant repertoire

- "Ga-ga-ga" with giraffe animations
- "Na-na-na" with nose-touching games
- "Wa-wa-wa" with water splash effects
Baby experiments with new sounds

### Week 19-20: Babbling Chains
## Learning Objective: Create longer sound sequences

- Extended babbling: "bababababa" with chain animations
- Long sound sequences with continuous visuals
- Rhythmic babbling with beat patterns
Baby produces longer sound chains

### Week 21-22: Mixed Sound Patterns
## Learning Objective: Vary consonant-vowel combinations

- "Ba-da-ga" with different character appearances
- Mixed patterns with changing colors/shapes
- Varied sequences with surprise elements
Baby creates mixed babbling patterns

### Week 23-24: Intonation & Melody
## Learning Objective: Add musical qualities to sounds

- Question sounds with rising visual arrows
- Excited sounds with energetic animations
- Gentle sounds with calm, flowing visuals
Baby uses different tones in vocalizations

## Level 3: Word Recognition & Meaning (6-9 Months)
### Week 25-26: Name Recognition
## Learning Objective: Respond to own name

- Baby's name appears with special animations
- "Where's [name]?" hide-and-seek games
- Name songs with personalized characters
Baby turns toward screen when name is called

### Week 27-28: First Meaningful Words
## Learning Objective: Understand "Mama" and "Dada"

- Mother and father characters with voice labels
- "Mama" and "Dada" with pointing animations
- Recognition games with parent figures
Baby attempts meaningful word sounds

### Week 29-30: Action Word Recognition
## Learning Objective: Connect words with actions

- "Up!" with lifting animations
- "More!" with adding/increasing visuals
- "Bye-bye!" with waving characters
Baby associates words with movements

### Week 31-32: Object Naming
## Learning Objective: Connect words with objects

- "Ball" with bouncing ball animations
- "Book" with page-turning visuals
- "Cup" with drinking motions
Baby looks at objects when named

### Week 33-34: Animal Sound Recognition
## Learning Objective: Identify animal sounds

- "Moo" with cow animations
- "Woof" with dog characters
- "Meow" with cat movements
Baby attempts animal sound imitations

### Week 35-36: Social Word Understanding
## Learning Objective: Recognize social expressions

- "Hi!" with friendly waving characters
- "Thank you" with giving/receiving animations
- "Please" with polite gesture visuals
Baby responds to social cues

## Level 4: Word Production & Simple Understanding (9-12 Months)
### Week 37-38: Clear Word Attempts
## Learning Objective: Produce recognizable words

- Clear "Mama" and "Dada" with mouth close-ups
- Pronunciation practice with slow-motion visuals
- Word clarity games with rewards
Baby produces clearer word approximations

### Week 39-40: Request Communication
## Learning Objective: Use words to ask for things

- "Up!" when character wants to be lifted
- "More!" during feeding game scenarios
- "Help!" with problem-solving animations
Baby uses sounds to request things

### Week 41-42: Simple Command Understanding
## Learning Objective: Follow basic instructions

- "Come here!" with approaching animations
- "Sit down" with sitting character demos
- "Give me" with hand-reaching visuals
Baby responds to simple commands

### Week 43-44: Descriptive Word Recognition
## Learning Objective: Understand describing words

- "Big" and "little" with size comparisons
- "Hot" and "cold" with temperature visuals
- "Fast" and "slow" with speed demonstrations
Baby shows understanding through reactions

### Week 45-46: Location Word Understanding
## Learning Objective: Understand spatial concepts

- "Here" and "there" with distance animations
- "Up" and "down" with directional movements
- "In" and "out" with container demonstrations
Baby follows location-based instructions

### Week 47-48: Emotion Word Recognition
## Learning Objective: Identify feeling words

- "Happy" with smiling character animations
- "Sad" with crying character visuals
- "Love" with heart and hug animations
Baby shows emotional responses to words

### Week 49-50: Two-Word Understanding
## Learning Objective: Process word combinations

- "Mama up!" with mother lifting scenarios
- "More milk!" with feeding animations
- "Bye-bye Dada!" with father leaving scenes
Baby understands simple two-word phrases

### Week 51-52: Advanced Communication
## Learning Objective: Complex sound-meaning connections

- Review all learned words with interactive games
- Combination activities mixing all previous skills
- Celebration animations for achievements
Baby demonstrates comprehensive understanding

## Assessment & Progress Tracking
### Monthly Milestones:

- Month 1: Responds to sounds and voices
- Month 2: Makes cooing sounds
- Month 3: Produces varied vowel sounds
- Month 4: Creates consonant-vowel combinations
- Month 5: Engages in sound exchanges
- Month 6: Uses intonation patterns
- Month 7: Responds to name consistently
- Month 8: Attempts first words
- Month 9: Uses sounds communicatively
- Month 10: Produces 2-3 recognizable words
- Month 11: Follows simple instructions
- Month 12: Combines words meaningfully

## Timings :

- 10-15 minute focused learning sessions
- Multiple short 2-3 minute activities throughout day
- Gentle background sounds during quiet time
- Interactive response-based games
