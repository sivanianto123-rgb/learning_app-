## SYLLABUS FOR PHONICS

##Single Letter Sounds (Consonants)

 b - /b/ (bat, boy)
 c - /k/ (cat, cup) or /s/ (city, cent)
 d - /d/ (dog, day)
 f - /f/ (fish, fun)
 g - /g/ (go, get) or /j/ (gem, giant)
 h - /h/ (hat, home)
 j - /j/ (jump, jam)
 k - /k/ (kite, key)
 l - /l/ (love, let)
  m - /m/ (mat, mom)
  n - /n/ (net, nose)
 p - /p/ (pet, pop)
 q - /kw/ (queen, quit)
 r -  /r/ (run, red)
 s - /s/ (sun, sit)
 t - /t/ (top, ten)
 v - /v/ (van, very)
 w - /w/ (win, wet)
 x - /ks/ (box, six)
 y - /y/ (yes, you) or /i/ (gym, myth)
 z - /z/ (zoo, zip)

##Short Vowel Sounds

 a - /æ/ (cat, bat, hat)
 e - /ɛ/ (bed, red, pen)
 i - /ɪ/ (sit, bit, pin)
 o - /ɒ/ (hot, pot, dog)
 u - /ʌ/ (cup, bug, run)

##Long Vowel Sounds

 a - /eɪ/ (cake, name, day)

 i - /aɪ/ (kite, bike, my)
 o - /oʊ/ (boat, go, snow)
 u - /u/ (cube, tune, few)

 th - /θ/ (thin, math) or /ð/ (this, that)
 wh - /w/ (when, where)
 ph - /f/ (phone, graph)
 ck - /k/ (back, stick)
 ng - /ŋ/ (ring, song)
 nk - /ŋk/ (think, bank)

## R-Controlled Vowels

##Silent Letter Patterns

##WORD LEARNING SYLLABUS
###Level 1: Foundation Sound
 ####Week 1-2: Single Letter Sounds

 	a, t, p, i, n, s
	Practice: at, it, pat, pit, tap, tip, sit, sip

 ####Week 3-4: More Single Sounds

	m, d, g, o, c, k
	Practice: mad, dog, got, cod, mop, top, kit, kid

 ####Week 5-6: Remaining Single Sounds

	e, u, r, h, b, f, l
	Practice: red, bed, bug, hug, but, fun, let, leg

 ####Week 7-8: Final Single Sounds

	j, v, w, x, y, z
	Practice: jet, van, win, box, yes, zip

##Level 2: Simple Word Building (Ages 5-6)
 ####Week 9-10: CVC Words (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant)

	Short a: cat, bat, rat, hat, mat, fat, sat
	Short e: bed, red, pen, ten, hen, men, wet
	Short i: bit, hit, sit, fit, pin, win, big
	Short o: hot, pot, dot, got, hop, top, dog
	Short u: cup, cut, but, hut, run, sun, bug

 ####Week 11-12: Consonant Blends

	Beginning blends: bl, cl, fl, pl, br, cr, dr, fr, gr, pr, tr
	Words: blue, clap, flag, play, bread, crab, drum, frog, green, pray, tree

 ####Week 13-14: More Blends

	s-blends: sc, sk, sl, sm, sn, sp, st, sw
	Words: scan, skip, slip, smile, snap, spin, stop, swim

 ####Week 15-16: Ending Blends

	-nd, -nt, -st, -mp, -nk, -ng, -ck
	Words: hand, went, fast, jump, pink, ring, back

##Level 3: Complex Patterns (Ages 6-7)
 ##Week 17-18: Consonant Digraphs

	ch, sh, th, wh, ph, ck
	Words: chip, shop, thin, when, phone, duck

 ##Week 19-20: Long Vowels (Silent e)

	a_e: cake, make, take, name, game, same
	i_e: bike, like, time, nine, fine, mine
	o_e: home, bone, rope, hope, note, rode
	u_e: cute, tube, huge, tune, mule, rule

 ##Week 21-22: Vowel Teams

	ai/ay: rain, pain, day, play, say, way
	ee/ea: tree, see, eat, meat, read, team
	oa/ow: boat, coat, bow, grow, show, know

 ##Week 23-24: R-Controlled Vowels

	ar: car, star, park, hard, barn, farm
	or: for, born, corn, horn, short, sport
	er/ir/ur: her, bird, turn, girl, hurt, first

##Level 4: Advanced Patterns (Ages 7-8)
 ####Week 25-26: Complex Vowel Sounds

	ou/ow: out, house, cow, down, brown, found
	oi/oy: oil, coin, boy, toy, joy, point
	oo (long): moon, soon, food, room, cool, school
	oo (short): book, look, good, took, wood, foot

 ####Week 27-28: Silent Letters

	Silent b: lamb, thumb, climb, comb
	Silent k: knee, knife, know, knock
	Silent l: half, walk, talk, calm
	Silent w: write, wrong, wrist, wrap

 ####Week 29-30: Prefixes and Suffixes

	un-, re-, pre-: undo, remake, preview
	-ing, -ed, -er, -est: running, jumped, bigger, fastest
	-ly, -ful, -less: quickly, helpful, helpless


##PART B: SENTENCE LEARNING SYLLABUS
###Level 1: Basic Sentences (Ages 5-6)
 ####Week 1-2: Two-Word Sentences

	I am. I go. I see. I can.
	It is. We go. We see. We can.

 ####Week 3-4: Three-Word Sentences

	I can go. I see it. We can run.
	The cat sat. The dog ran. The sun is hot.

 ####Week 5-6: Simple Statements

	The cat is big. I can see you. We go to school.
	My mom is nice. The book is red. I like to play.

 ####Week 7-8: Questions and Answers

	What is it? It is a cat.
	Where do you go? I go home.
	Can you run? Yes, I can run.

##Level 2: Expanding Sentences (Ages 6-7)
 ####Week 9-10: Describing Words (Adjectives)

	The big dog runs fast. The little cat sleeps.
	I have a red ball. She wears a blue dress.
	The old man walks slowly. The young girl laughs.

 ####Week 11-12: Action Sentences

	The boy kicks the ball. The girl reads a book.
	Mom cooks dinner. Dad drives the car.
	We play in the park. They swim in the pool.

 ####Week 13-14: Time and Place Words

	Yesterday I went to school. Today we play outside.
	In the morning, I eat breakfast. At night, I sleep.
	We go to the store. They live in a house.

 ####Week 15-16: Compound Sentences

	I like apples, and she likes oranges.
	We want to play, but it is raining.
	You can walk, or you can ride your bike.

##Level 3: Complex Sentences (Ages 7-8)
 ####Week 17-18: Because and So

	I am happy because it is my birthday.
	It was raining, so we stayed inside.
	She studied hard because she wanted good grades.

 ####Week 19-20: When and While

	When I grow up, I want to be a teacher.
	While Mom cooked, I set the table.
	We will leave when Dad gets home.

 ####Week 21-22: Describing Sentences

	The beautiful butterfly flew over the colorful flowers.
	The brave firefighter rescued the scared cat from the tall tree.
	My grandmother baked delicious cookies for our family dinner.

 ####Week 23-24: Dialogue and Conversation

	"Hello," said Tom. "How are you today?"
	"I am fine," replied Sara. "Thank you for asking."
	The teacher asked, "Did you finish your homework?"

##Level 4: Advanced Sentences (Ages 8-9)
 ####Week 25-26: Story Sentences

	Once upon a time, there lived a kind princess in a beautiful castle.
	First, we gathered all the ingredients. Then, we mixed them together.
	Finally, after a long journey, they reached their destination safely.

 ####Week 27-28: Opinion and Reason

	I think dogs make the best pets because they are loyal and friendly.
	In my opinion, reading is important because it helps us learn new things.
	Many people believe that exercise is good for your health.

 ####Week 29-30: Creative Writing

	If I could fly, I would soar above the clouds and see the world below.
	The mysterious door creaked open, revealing a secret garden filled with magical creatures.
	As the sun set behind the mountains, the family gathered around the campfire to tell stories.
